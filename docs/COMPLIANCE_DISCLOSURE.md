# Compliance and evidence disclosure

This software is technical/research infrastructure, not a promise of profitability or
personal investment advice. Broker referral/turnover incentives must never enter model
objectives, admission gates or quality metrics. Frequency, lots and channel tags are
not evidence. Quality is measured in net R after full costs and independent OOS/live-
forward periods.

Telegram publication can be delayed and is not the source of truth. Broker order/deal
timestamps and the immutable internal trading-event ledger are authoritative. Historic
`#hehe/#nothehe` labels require explicit reply/time/symbol linkage to broker facts before
any analysis; unmatched messages remain descriptive archive records.
